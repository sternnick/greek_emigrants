# Glossary

[🇬🇷 Ελληνικά](glossary.el.md)

- **emigrant** — leaves their country. *Greek emigrants* = from Greece.
- **immigrant** — arrives in a country. *Greek immigrants in Australia* = from Australia's POV.
- **diaspora** — the whole population of Greek origin living abroad.
- **diaspora (GR)** — ελληνική διασπορά, απόδημος ελληνισμός.
- **confirmed** — backed by official statistics.
- **hypothetical** — estimate, not an official count.
- **definition** — what counts as "Greek" for a given record.
